The five plates are the path. The shelf is the menu. There is no order on this page, and there is no prize for installing all of it.
I have watched operators finish the task audit, feel the momentum, and reach for a blank file to build something that already sits on this shelf. Do not be that person. Match the shelf against the job that is still on you, take what fits, and leave the rest.

How it works today

You already installed the community plugin — skyremote/skool-navaigate-install — when you did Install the crew, or you clone it and run ./install.sh. The modules are skills on that plugin. You type /aios, you pick, and the matching command runs in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.
Your keys live in ~/.claude/.env. Firecrawl, Exa, Gemini, Supadata — those are yours. We do not ship mine.

What is on the shelf

  • Thumbnails — four concepts from your photos, headline composited after the image. /thumbnails
  • Diagrams — D2 in plain English, render, look, iterate. /diagrams
  • Web scrape — Exa to find, Firecrawl to extract, plus Reddit, papers, Substack, X. /web-scrape
  • Podcasts — find the episode, rank it, pull the words. /podcasts
  • Transcripts — YouTube, TikTok, IG. Paid, metered. /transcripts
  • Deep research — multi-source pass, not one search box. /deep-research
  • Writing style — anti-slop on the draft. Your voice is /your-voice. /writing-style
  • New app — plan, then phases, sequential. /new-app
  • New capability — talk to a tool we did not ship. /new-capability
  • Frontend design — a page that does not look generated. /frontend-design
  • Your AIOS coach — a tab that already knows this course. You put it online. /aios-coach

Design Brain, Content Coach and Hormozi are already later in this course. They are on the same /aios pick list.
Type /aios and name the job. We will not walk you through every module. We will run the one you picked.
