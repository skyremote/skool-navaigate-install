Sometimes you do not want a search. You have the URL. You want the words — the YouTube talk, the TikTok, the Instagram live, the file sitting on the machine. That is this module.
It runs on Supadata. Paid, credit-metered. Put SUPADATA_API_KEY in ~/.claude/.env and type /transcripts with the link. You get the full transcript, not a recap, and then we do the job you asked: decisions, objections, quotes.
If you do not have a URL yet and you want episodes found and ranked, that is /podcasts. If you want the open web, that is /web-scrape.
Type /aios and pick the next one.

When you want this in a tab of your own, type /aios-coach. You build it, you put it online, your key. It already knows this course. It is not on navaigate.dev yet.
