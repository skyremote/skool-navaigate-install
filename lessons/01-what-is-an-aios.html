An AIOS is an operating system for how you run the business with agents, and I want to be precise, because the phrase gets used for a chatbot with a folder next to it.
Windows runs the computer. Your AIOS runs the work. It is not a clever prompt you saved, and it is not another forty-nine-dollar tab you open twice. It is your context, your numbers, your meetings, the jobs you actually repeat, and the builds you keep — five plates, one thread through them.

What that looks like when it is running

  • It knows the business, so you stop re-explaining yourself every chat.
  • It can see the numbers you actually use, not seven dashboards you assemble in your head.
  • It watches the meetings and the messages and puts a brief where you will read it.
  • It lives in the harness you already open — Claude, Cursor, Codex, or Grok Bot.
  • It does the repeatable jobs so your hours go back to the work that moves something.

Fifteen modules. After every one you type /aios and it asks where you are — empty, halfway, or already running — and which lesson you are in. That is the gold thread on the cover.
I run NavAIgate, BluPlai and this classroom on that shape. Chief of Staff 0.4.2 is the crew. Design Brain is 1,119 sourced entries. The chart gallery is 19 charts you actually open. My voice file, my brain search and my invoices stay on my machines. You will build yours.
Type /aios and tell it you just finished what an AIOS is.
