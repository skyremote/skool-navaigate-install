You started a business to be free, and then somewhere along the way it inverted. You check dashboards before you have finished your coffee, and you sit in meetings whose only job is to keep you across things, and you have quietly become the highest-paid administrator in your own company.
That is the operator trap. You are the integration layer. Every tool dumps a job back on you, and you call that being across it.
We are not going to hire three people to manage the chaos, and we are not going to collect a drawer of downloads and call it a system. Context, numbers, meetings, tasks and builds sit on five plates, and a crew runs the repeatable bits.
If you recognise the Tuesday morning, stay on the path. If your week is already a system and you are only missing one plate, type /aios, say already running, and we fill the gap. We do not wipe a working desk to make a clean demo.
