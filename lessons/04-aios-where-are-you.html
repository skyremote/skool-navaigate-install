Alright. Before we put anything on the five layers, we look at the folder you already have, because most of you are not starting from nothing, and I am not going to wipe a messy project to make a clean demo.
Type /aios in the harness you actually use and answer the two questions. It asks them at the start, and it asks them again after every layer, and that is deliberate.

Question 1 — where is the machine

  1. Empty. New folder. We start at layer 1 and we do not skip.
  2. Halfway. Notes, a half-written CLAUDE.md, a few abandoned folders. We do not delete that. We name what is already there and we fill the gap.
  3. Already running. You have a crew, or a voice, or a daily brief. We run status first. Then we only add the missing plate.

Question 2 — which lesson are you in

Your AIOS, Useful Resources, a recording from 01–55, Inner Circle gold, or one of the Level tracks. Say which, because the next command should open that page on your machine.

What we will not do

  • We will not install my voice, my brain search, or my invoices. Those stay on my machines. Later we build yours.
  • We will not dump a hundred skills into Claude because this Mac’s Cursor only carries five, and that is the honest shelf.

When it has those two answers, it will give you one next command. Do that, then come back here and type /aios again. If you ask a real question about the course — not just a pick — /aios answers from the twenty-six lessons we wrote. When you want that in a browser tab you host, that is /aios-coach.
