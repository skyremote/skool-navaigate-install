Listen to this lesson in my voice

You have spent weeks on the plates, and when someone asks how it actually works you reach for words and lose them around the third sentence. A diagram would have closed that in five seconds. The problem was never that diagrams are hard to read. It is that they were miserable to make.
This module installs D2 — a text diagram language — and then we draw in plain English. You describe the system. We write the D2, render a PNG, look at it, and fix the overlapping labels. That look-and-fix loop is the whole point. Plenty of tools can turn text into a diagram. Most of them cannot notice it is ugly.

Five starting patterns

  • Master overview — the five plates and what talks to what
  • Data pipeline — source, store, ask
  • Automation schedule — trigger, job, human checkpoint
  • Command system — harness, skill, outcome
  • Education or funnel — attention, lesson, install, result

No API key. D2 is free. Five to ten minutes to stand up, and most of that is the install.
Type /diagrams and point it at your AIOS, not a toy. Ask for a one-glance version for a client and an expanded one for you. When you can produce both in a few minutes, you will stop dreading the question.
