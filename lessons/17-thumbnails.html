Listen to this lesson in my voice

The thumbnail is the click. Nobody watches the edit, the script, the three hours in the booth — not until they have clicked — and they decide in less than a second. Get this wrong and the best video you have made dies in the feed.
This module takes the thumbnail off your critical path. Four concepts, side by side, in your look. You pick. You say "the second, warmer, face left." We refine. You ship.

What it actually does

You build a small catalogue of your photos once — wide-eyed, pointing, arms folded, mid-laugh — tagged by expression, pose and setting. When you ask for a thumbnail on a topic, we pull the photo that fits. Not my face. Not a camera-roll scroll at midnight.
Then two stages, because this is how it actually works on the machine today.

  • Stage 1 — the image model draws the scene. No words. Words come out misspelled if you let it write them.
  • Stage 2 — we composite the headline as a real text layer after. That is how it stays spelled correctly.

Four concepts fire in parallel, different compositions, same brand rules. You judge them against each other. You cannot judge a thumbnail in isolation.

What you need

A Gemini key in ~/.claude/.env as GEMINI_API_KEY. Your photo catalogue. Brand colours if you have them. Thirty to forty-five minutes on the catalogue the first time, and that pays back on every video.
Type /thumbnails with the video topic. You should get a 2x2. The tell that it is set up: your look holds across all four.
Type /aios and pick the next job.

When you want this in a tab of your own, type /aios-coach. You build it, you put it online, your key. It already knows this course. It is not on navaigate.dev yet.
