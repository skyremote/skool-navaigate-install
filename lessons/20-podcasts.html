Some of the sharpest thinking never gets written down. It gets said. An operator goes on a two-hour podcast, drops their guard, and tells you how they priced the first deal and where go-to-market broke. You will not find that in a blog post.
The problem is finding it. The episode you want is buried across Apple, Spotify, YouTube and a hundred show sites. This module gives the agent a way to find the right interviews, rank them, and pull the full transcript of the best ones.

Two nets, because podcasts live in two places

Wide net — Exa and Firecrawl search the open web, which is how you catch Apple, Spotify and the long tail of show sites.
Deep net — Supadata searches YouTube, where the metadata actually lives: views, duration, date. You can ask for interviews over forty minutes from the last six months.
Then we rank. View count, duration, whether the show actually interviews operators. Best three at the top, with why. Then we pull the full transcript, not a summary. A summary decides for you what mattered. The transcript keeps the specifics, and the specifics are why you went looking.
Supadata is paid and metered. Put SUPADATA_API_KEY next to your Firecrawl and Exa keys. Keep the first pass small.
Type /podcasts and ask for the episodes on the thing you are actually building this week. Type /aios when you have the words.
