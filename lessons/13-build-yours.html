Listen to this lesson in my voice

There are a few things on my machines that look like magic from the outside, and they are not magic, and you cannot have the files, because they are mine. What you can have is the process, and then a skill that is yours.
Same pattern as the GrokBot templates Matt walked — you build it once, you can share the pattern, and the secrets stay out of the share.

Your voice — not mine

I have a voice skill trained on 54 community calls and then line-edited by me. That file does not leave this desk. What we give you is the builder. You point it at your recordings, your sent mail, the lines you actually rewrote. Type /your-voice. The public kit is github.com/skyremote/voice-skill-builder. You do not install daniel-voice.

Your memory — not my search

I can ask an old Claude or Cursor or Codex chat what we decided last week, and I get a short pack back — not a dumped transcript. That database is not a community download. Type /your-brain. If you already have notes in Obsidian or a folder of transcripts, we start there. We do not make you adopt my cloud.

Your invoices — not my company

I have a skill that fills a branded invoice for NavAIgate. You do not get that template. You would be invoicing as the wrong company. Type /your-ops. Bring one real invoice you sent this year. We componentize that, and we stop.
If a skill has my name, my register number, my call recordings, or my old chats in it, it stays here. If it is a process you can run on your material, it belongs in the community system.
Pick one of the three this week. Then type /aios and say which one you started.

When you want this in a tab of your own, type /aios-coach. You build it, you put it online, your key. It already knows this course. It is not on navaigate.dev yet.
