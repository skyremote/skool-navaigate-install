# Course pack

`your-aios.json` holds the titles and Skool links of the twenty-six Your AIOS
lessons, plus the community skill fronts. That is what `/aios` and `/aios-coach` know.
The lesson text lives on Skool only.

It is not a live scrape of Skool. Do not log in and pull the classroom
to refresh it. Rebuild from `skool-navaigate-install/lessons/` if the
copy changes.

GPLS is a different product. This pack does not include it.
