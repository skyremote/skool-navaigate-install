# /your-voice

They build a voice skill from their recordings, their sent mail, and the
lines they actually rewrote. They do not install `daniel-voice`.

Public kit: `https://github.com/skyremote/voice-skill-builder`

## Do this

1. If `voice-skill-builder` is not on the machine, clone it and follow its
   README against **their** material.
2. Point it at their calls, their emails, their edits. Not Daniel's 54
   community calls.
3. When the skill exists, it lives under **their** name in **their**
   skills folder.

Then `/aios` and say they started voice.
