# Social Content Psychology

Use this method when the goal is content that earns attention, trust and a relevant next step.

## The sequence

1. **See me** — name the private frustration, insecurity or desire the right person is already carrying.
2. **Teach it** — explain one mechanism in plain language.
3. **Prove it** — show a personal result, customer result, worked example or live demonstration.
4. **Offer the next step** — give one useful action or one relevant offer.

The content should demonstrate the lesson while it teaches it. Do not just say that recognition matters; begin by making the viewer feel recognised.

## Weekly use

Start with:

> What are you doing this week?

Then provide the audience, offer, private problem, proof and platform. Ask `/content-coach` to return:

- the private tension;
- a curiosity hook and open loop;
- one mechanism to teach;
- the demonstration or proof;
- one clear CTA; and
- a talk track for speaking over the content.

The method combines the value and proof discipline of Ask Hormozi, the visible founder perspective of James Smith, and the strongest recurring pattern from the social-video research library: make the viewer feel specifically seen and understood.

Do not invent results, testimonials or numbers. If proof is limited, say so.
