Listen to this lesson in my voice

Layer 3 is intelligence, and most of it happens out loud and then vanishes. You had a call on Tuesday where a decision actually got made, and a client said the one sentence that explains why the deal stalled, and by Friday you cannot find it. Memory feels like a system of record because it is free. It is a terrible one.
We close that gap in three pieces, and they have to land in order.

1. Capture the meeting

I use Granola on calls. You can use Fireflies, Fathom, or a folder of transcripts you already export. I am not fussy about the vendor. I am fussy that the words land in a place the machine can see. If you have nothing, start with Granola or export last week's calls into this project. Type /layer-3 and tell it which one you actually use. If the folder is empty, we write the smallest capture path. If you are halfway, we do not rip out what is there.

2. A brief you will actually read

Collection is not thinking. The brief is the "so what". It has to sit where you will see it — a file in this project, not a seventh tab you will never open. The rule is simple and it is the whole game: the brief cannot synthesise what it cannot see. Capture first, then the brief.

3. Something always-on in your pocket

Grok Bot is the always-on. Call 54 was the operating note: own computer, phone, receipts can run at 04:00, and coding stays in a coding harness. Hermes is the tinkery path if you like building the bot. We do not install OpenClaw.
If you want the specialist templates, Useful Resources already has that post — you build a specialist once, you share a pattern, secrets stay out. That is the walk from the community call. Type /layer-3 and say if you want the pocket as well.

The research tools sit on this plate too

Reading the live web, pulling a podcast transcript, searching Reddit or papers — that is still intelligence. It is not a separate course. After this lesson you type /aios and you pick: /web-scrape, /podcasts, /transcripts, /deep-research. Those modules are on the shelf. They install through Claude Code, with your keys, not a zip.
Type /aios when the brief would make sense to you on a Wednesday morning.

When you want this in a tab of your own, type /aios-coach. You build it, you put it online, your key. It already knows this course. It is not on navaigate.dev yet.
