# Writing a video prompt shot by shot

## The four sections, every time

**1. SHOT-BY-SHOT EFFECTS TIMELINE**
```
SHOT 1 (0:00-0:02) — Shot name
• EFFECT: primary effect + any stacked effects
• What happens on screen, plainly
• Camera: angle, movement, lens if it matters
• Speed or timing ("approximately 25% speed")
• How it exits into the next shot
```
Shots run back to back with no gaps. Mark one shot "SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT": the one people remember.

**2. MASTER EFFECTS INVENTORY.** Every distinct effect, how many times it is used, in which shots, and its job.

**3. EFFECTS DENSITY MAP.** Three to six second chunks rated HIGH (4+ effects), MEDIUM (2 to 3) or LOW (1 or clean).

**4. ENERGY ARC.** How it grabs attention, how it builds, how it lands.

## Shot counts by length

| Length | Shots | Signature effects |
|---|---|---|
| up to 10 s | 4 to 7 | 1 |
| 10 to 20 s | 8 to 14 | 1 or 2 |
| 20 to 30 s | 12 to 20 | 2 or 3 |

No length given: 15 to 20 seconds.

## Principles

1. Contrast drives impact: a slow shot after a fast one hits harder than two fast ones.
2. Transitions are shots, not throwaway joins.
3. Be specific: "rotates clockwise about 15 degrees", not "tilts".
4. Describe the result on screen, not editing-software steps.
5. Land the ending on purpose.
6. No hype words. Say what happens.

## Images

Subject, action, scene, camera, light, style, then what to avoid. Light is the biggest single lever: name the
source, the direction and the time of day. Use the member's own product photos as references.
