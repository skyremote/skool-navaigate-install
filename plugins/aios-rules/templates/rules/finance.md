# Finance folder

- Invoices are named `YYYY-MM-DD_client_invoice-NNN_v1.pdf`. Never reuse an invoice number.
- Check the total, the tax line and the due date against the source before you call an invoice done.
- Never write bank logins, card numbers or tax IDs into a note. Link to where they live instead.
