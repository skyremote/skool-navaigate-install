# Prompts: tools-offers

Paste one into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. Change the business details to your own.

## 1. Check what you have

```text
Use tools-offers. Check whether the Hormozi Growth Engine and my corpus are set up, and tell me exactly what to do next. Do not answer any Hormozi question until it is set up.
```

**What it does:** runs `doctor` and lists what is installed and what is missing.
**How you know it worked:** you get a short list: engine installed or the clone command, corpus pointed or not, and
the James Smith skill optional.

## 2. Fix your offer, cited

```text
Use tools-offers. Fix my offer: Northwind Bakery sells a GBP 29 monthly box (a loaf, two pastries, a jar of jam) and people cancel after two months. Cite book, chapter and page for every change, and say plainly where you cannot cite.
```

**What it does:** routes to the Offer Enhancer or Grand Slam Offer Builder and reworks the offer from the books.
**How you know it worked:** every change names the book, the chapter and the page from your own corpus, or is labelled as the
engine's summary because your corpus does not cover it yet.

## 3. A second view on pricing

```text
Use tools-offers and the James Smith skill. I charge GBP 300 a month for coaching, 20 clients, and people say it is too expensive. How would James Smith look at raising the price? Cite the video, and keep what he says separate from your advice.
```

**What it does:** pulls the relevant James Smith videos and applies them to your numbers.
**How you know it worked:** each point names a video, and the answer has a clear "what the source says" and "how it
applies to you" split.
