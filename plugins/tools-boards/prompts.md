# Prompts: tools-boards

Paste one into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor.

## 1. Your first board, from the practice scene

```text
Use tools-boards. Make the practice scenes in ~/Boards-practice, validate the workshop one, then build it as a new BluPlai board called "Practice: Northwind kick-off" and read it back to me.
```

**What it does:** validates a fictional workshop runway, writes it to your BluPlai account and reads it back.
**How you know it worked:** you get the board link, 17 shapes and 4 arrows, and when you open it every box can be
dragged on its own.

## 2. A transcript into a board people can talk on

```text
Use tools-boards. Turn this workshop transcript into a BluPlai board with the five themes, what people said under each, and the actions with owners, with space for the team to add notes: <paste transcript or file path>
```

**What it does:** pulls the themes, quotes and actions from the transcript and lays them out left to right.
**How you know it worked:** every quote on the board is from the transcript, every action names an owner said in
the call, and there is a marked space to write back.

## 3. Match a screenshot, but editable

```text
Use tools-boards. Rebuild the diagram in this screenshot as an editable BluPlai board with real shapes and arrows, not an image: <attach screenshot>
```

**What it does:** reads the structure of the picture and rebuilds it as native shapes.
**How you know it worked:** the board has no image on it, the boxes move independently, and the arrows stay attached.
